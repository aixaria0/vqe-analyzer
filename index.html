FROM A FIRST REPOSITORY, FORWARD.

Small enough to understand.
Real enough to explore.

VQE Analyzer is Aria Fani’s quantum optimization workbench. It runs small, noiseless statevector experiments entirely on your device.

Live evolution preserves better circuit parameters between trials and adapts its mutation radius. Every new run starts with fresh randomness. Learning memory is kept on this device, not sent to a server.

Energy is evaluated from the actual circuit state. Adam and gradient descent use exact parameter-shift derivatives; SPSA uses two perturbed evaluations per step. Reference energies come from exact diagonalization.

This is an educational simulator. It does not execute on quantum hardware, model device noise, or guarantee the ansatz reaches the ground state. H₂ reports electronic energy without nuclear repulsion. The portfolio is a toy objective.
